# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "MNDA") is entered into between {{Party A Legal Name}} ("Party A") and {{Party B Legal Name}} ("Party B") on {{Effective Date}} (the "Effective Date"). Each of Party A and Party B is a "Party" and together they are the "Parties".

---

## 1. Plain-language summary

_Plain-language: this section is a non-binding overview. The clauses below are the binding terms._

This MNDA is a two-way confidentiality agreement. Either Party may share confidential information with the other and either Party may receive it. The Party sharing in a given instance is the "Discloser"; the Party receiving is the "Recipient". The same Party may be Discloser for some items and Recipient for others.

Confidential Information is defined broadly: anything shared for the Purpose that a reasonable person would treat as confidential, whether spoken, written, shown, or shared in any other form, and whether or not labelled. It is not Confidential Information if Recipient already had it without restriction, if it is or becomes public through no fault of Recipient, if a third party shared it lawfully without restriction, or if Recipient developed it independently.

The obligations have two clocks. The Disclosure Period is the window during which the Parties may share Confidential Information under this MNDA. The Confidentiality Term is how long the duty to protect a given item lasts after it is shared. Trade secrets are protected for as long as they remain trade secrets under applicable law.

When the discussion ends, Recipient returns or destroys what it received, with narrow carve-outs for routine backups and legal-hold copies. Nothing here grants a licence to the other Party's intellectual property, and money damages alone may not be enough to remedy a breach.

## 3. Definitions

_Plain-language: capitalised terms used in this MNDA have the meanings given here. Plain meanings apply elsewhere._

For the purposes of this MNDA:

- "Confidential Information" has the meaning in Section 4.
- "Discloser" means the Party (or its Representatives) sharing Confidential Information in a given instance.
- "Recipient" means the Party (or its Representatives) receiving Confidential Information in a given instance.
- "Representatives" means a Party's employees, officers, directors, professional advisers, agents, and contractors who (i) need to know the Confidential Information for the Purpose and (ii) are bound by written confidentiality obligations no less protective than this MNDA.
- "Effective Date" means the date stated above (or, if different, the date the last Party signs).
- "Disclosure Period" has the meaning in Section 9.
- "Trade Secret" means information meeting the legal definition of a trade secret under applicable law, including (where applicable) the U.S. Defend Trade Secrets Act, EU Directive 2016/943, and the UK Trade Secrets (Enforcement, etc.) Regulations 2018.

## 4. Confidential Information

_Plain-language: any non-public information shared for the Purpose is covered, whether or not it is labelled._

Confidential Information means any non-public information that Discloser makes available to Recipient in connection with the Purpose, in any form (oral, written, visual, electronic, tangible, or intangible), whether or not marked or otherwise designated as confidential, that a reasonable person in Recipient's position would understand to be confidential given the nature of the information and the circumstances of disclosure.

Confidential Information includes, without limitation: technical information (designs, specifications, source code, algorithms, models, datasets, security architecture, vulnerability details); business information (strategies, roadmaps, forecasts, pricing, costs, marketing plans, unannounced products); financial information; customer, supplier, and personnel information; and the existence, status, and contents of the Parties' discussions relating to the Purpose.

Confidential Information does not need to be marked "confidential" to be protected, but Discloser is encouraged to mark items where practicable to reduce later disputes.

---

## 5. Permitted disclosures & use

_Plain-language: Recipient may use the information only for the Purpose, and may share it only with people who actually need to see it._

Recipient shall use Discloser's Confidential Information solely for the Purpose. Recipient shall not use Confidential Information to reverse-engineer Discloser's products or to develop products competitive with Discloser, except to the limited extent expressly permitted by this MNDA.

Recipient may disclose Confidential Information only to its Representatives who (a) need to know it for the Purpose and (b) are bound by written confidentiality obligations no less protective than this MNDA (or, for professional advisers, by professional duties of confidentiality of at least equivalent stringency).

Recipient is responsible for any act or omission of its Representatives that, if undertaken by Recipient, would constitute a breach of this MNDA. Recipient shall promptly notify Discloser in writing of any actual or suspected unauthorised use or disclosure of which it becomes aware, and shall reasonably cooperate to mitigate the effects.

---

## 6. Standard of care

_Plain-language: protect the other side's information at least as carefully as you protect your own — and never less than reasonably._

Recipient shall protect Confidential Information using at least the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential information of similar importance, and in any event no less than a reasonable standard of care. This includes administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the nature and sensitivity of the Confidential Information.

---

## 7. Exclusions

_Plain-language: information that is already public, already known, independently developed, or lawfully obtained from a third party is not protected._

The obligations in this MNDA do not apply to information that Recipient can demonstrate by contemporaneous written or electronic records:

- (a) was already in Recipient's possession, without confidentiality obligation, before being received from Discloser;
- (b) is or becomes publicly available through no act or omission of Recipient or its Representatives in breach of this MNDA;
- (c) was lawfully received from a third party without confidentiality obligation and without breach by that third party of any duty owed to Discloser; or
- (d) was independently developed by or for Recipient without reference to or use of Discloser's Confidential Information.

These exclusions do not, in themselves, give Recipient any right to disclose, publish, or otherwise exploit information that may also constitute personal data, regulated data, or a Trade Secret; obligations under such law continue on their own terms.

---

## 8. Compelled disclosure

_Plain-language: if a court or regulator forces disclosure, give the other side notice (where allowed) and only hand over what the law actually requires._

If Recipient becomes legally compelled — by subpoena, court order, statute, regulation, or governmental, regulatory, or self-regulatory authority — to disclose Confidential Information, Recipient shall (to the extent legally permitted) give Discloser prompt written notice sufficient to allow Discloser to seek a protective order or other appropriate remedy, and shall provide reasonable cooperation in that effort at Discloser's expense.

If a protective order or equivalent remedy is not obtained, Recipient shall disclose only the portion legally required, shall use commercially reasonable efforts to obtain reliable assurance of confidential treatment, and shall continue to treat the remainder as Confidential Information.

Nothing here prevents Recipient or its Representatives from making a good-faith report to, or cooperating with, a governmental authority to the extent protected under applicable law (including whistleblower protections).

## 9. Term & duration of obligations

_Plain-language: there are three clocks — how long you can share, how long protection lasts after sharing, and a separate clock for trade secrets._

This MNDA takes effect on the Effective Date.

The Parties may exchange Confidential Information during the Disclosure Period of {{Disclosure Period}} (default: two (2) years from the Effective Date). Either Party may end the Disclosure Period at any time on written notice.

Recipient's obligations of confidentiality and non-use for each item continue for the Confidentiality Term of {{Confidentiality Term}} (default: five (5) years from the date that item was disclosed).

For Confidential Information that constitutes a Trade Secret, Recipient's obligations continue for {{Trade-Secret Term}} (default: for so long as the information remains a Trade Secret under applicable law). Where applicable law requires a longer minimum period (for example, for personal data or regulated data), that longer period applies.

Termination or expiration of the Disclosure Period does not relieve Recipient of obligations accrued beforehand, nor of obligations that by their nature survive (including those in Sections 5 to 13).

## 10. Return or destruction of materials

_Plain-language: when asked, give it back or delete it — with sensible carve-outs for backups and legal copies._

On Discloser's written request, or on expiration or termination of the Disclosure Period, Recipient shall — at Discloser's option — return or destroy all Confidential Information of Discloser in Recipient's or its Representatives' possession or control, including copies, extracts, and derivatives. Recipient shall complete the return or destruction within {{Return Window}} (default: thirty (30) days) and, on request, certify destruction in writing.

Recipient is not required to delete or destroy:

- (a) copies retained automatically in standard backup, archival, disaster-recovery, or business-continuity systems, provided such copies are not accessed for operational purposes and are deleted or overwritten in the ordinary course;
- (b) one (1) copy retained by Recipient's legal counsel, or by a Representative subject to a legal-hold or regulatory record-keeping obligation, solely to evidence compliance with this MNDA or with that obligation; and
- (c) information stored solely in the unaided memory of authorised Representatives.

Confidential Information retained under this Section 10 remains subject to the obligations of this MNDA for as long as it is retained, regardless of any expiration of the Confidentiality Term.

## 11. No license; ownership

_Plain-language: receiving information is not the same as getting a licence — Discloser keeps all of its IP._

As between the Parties, Confidential Information remains the property of Discloser. Nothing in this MNDA grants, by implication, estoppel, or otherwise, any right or licence under any patent, copyright, trade-mark, trade secret, database right, or other intellectual property right of Discloser, except the limited right to use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose during the Disclosure Period.

No Party is obligated to disclose any particular information, to enter into any further agreement, or to refrain from dealings with third parties (including competitors of the other Party), subject only to the express obligations of this MNDA. Each Party acknowledges that the other may now or in the future develop products or services that compete with its own; this MNDA does not restrict either Party's general business activities, except as expressly stated.

## 13. Remedies & equitable relief

_Plain-language: damages alone may not be enough — courts can order the breaching party to stop._

Each Party acknowledges that breach of this MNDA may cause the other irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be inadequate. In addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity, Discloser may seek injunctive relief, specific performance, and other equitable remedies — without posting a bond or proving actual damages — to prevent or restrain a threatened or actual breach.

The remedies in this MNDA are cumulative and not exclusive. No election of one remedy excludes any other. Nothing here limits a Party's right to recover damages or other relief permitted by applicable law, subject to any limitation of liability separately agreed in writing.

## 16. Miscellaneous

_Plain-language: standard housekeeping clauses — entire agreement, no waiver, severability, assignment, counterparts, e-signatures._

**Entire agreement.** This MNDA, together with Annex A and any signed jurisdictional supplement, is the entire agreement between the Parties on its subject matter and supersedes all prior or contemporaneous understandings on that subject matter. It does not modify any other agreement between the Parties on a different subject matter.

**No waiver.** A Party's failure or delay in exercising any right does not waive that right. A waiver is effective only if in writing, signed by the waiving Party, and applies only to the specific instance and purpose for which it is given.

**Severability.** If any provision is held invalid, illegal, or unenforceable, that provision shall be modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable while preserving the Parties' original intent, and the remaining provisions continue in full force.

**Assignment.** Neither Party may assign this MNDA, by operation of law or otherwise, without the other Party's prior written consent, except that either Party may assign without consent to a successor in connection with a merger, acquisition, corporate reorganisation, or sale of all or substantially all of its assets relating to the Purpose, provided the successor agrees in writing to be bound. Any purported assignment in breach is void.

**Independent contractors.** The Parties are independent contractors. This MNDA does not create any partnership, joint venture, agency, fiduciary, or employment relationship.

**No publicity.** Neither Party shall use the other's name, logo, or trade marks, or disclose the existence or contents of the discussions or this MNDA, in any public statement or marketing material, without the other Party's prior written consent, except as required by Section 8.

**Counterparts; electronic signatures.** This MNDA may be signed in counterparts, each an original and together one instrument. The Parties consent to execution by electronic signature, including signatures that comply with eIDAS Regulation (EU) No 910/2014, the U.S. ESIGN Act and UETA, the UK Electronic Communications Act 2000, and equivalent regimes elsewhere. An electronic or scanned signature has the same effect as a handwritten one.

**Headings.** Headings are for convenience only.
